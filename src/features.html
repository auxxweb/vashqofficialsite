FEATURES

JOB CREATION AND ASSIGNING

Every job, started right and handed to the right hands.

Create a job in moments and assign it to the right team member instantly. Every vehicle has a live status, so nothing slips through, even on your busiest day.

  • Quick entry for walk-ins and bookings
  • Clear ownership of every job
  • Live progress across the wash floor
NUMBER PLATE SCAN

Recognition at the gate. Not data entry.

Scan the plate and Vashq identifies the vehicle at once. Returning customers appear with their details and history, so check-in feels effortless for them and for you.

  • Instant vehicle and customer lookup
  • Fewer errors at entry
  • A faster, more polished first impression
ADD SERVICE

Every extra, captured and billed.

When a customer wants more, add it to the running job in a tap. The bill updates itself, so no service goes unrecorded and no revenue is left behind.

  • Add services without restarting the job
  • Automatic bill updates
  • A complete record of the work done
PRODUCT QUALITY CHECK

Excellence, confirmed before it leaves.

Add a final checkpoint before handover to make sure every vehicle meets your standard. Catch issues early and protect the reputation you’ve built.

  • A built-in review before delivery
  • Consistent quality across your team
  • Fewer complaints
EMPLOYEE ATTENDANCE

Your team, accounted for. Effortlessly.

Track attendance and shifts digitally and see how your day is staffed at a glance. Replace registers and guesswork with accurate, organised records.

  • Digital attendance for every employee
  • A clear view of daily coverage
  • Reliable records for payroll and planning
CUSTOMERS AND LEADS

Every customer known. Every enquiry followed up.

Keep customer details, enquiries and leads in one organised space. Respond at the right moment and turn first-time interest into loyal, repeat business.

  • A single home for customer information
  • Enquiry and lead tracking
  • Timely follow-ups that convert
CUSTOMER HISTORY

A memory for every customer, every visit.

See each customer’s past services and bills in a single view. Recommend the right service, answer questions with confidence and make every return visit feel personal.

  • Full service and billing history
  • Smarter recommendations for repeat customers
  • A more personal experience that builds trust
PACKAGES

Smart packages. Effortless upselling.

Create wash and detailing packages to suit every customer and budget. Pitch the right one during job creation and add it in a single tap.

  • Flexible packages for every need
  • Natural upselling at the point of service
  • Clear, consistent pricing and higher value per visit
ONLINE APPOINTMENT BOOKING

Open for bookings, around the clock.

Let customers choose their service and time slot online, whenever it suits them. Bookings land in Vashq automatically, so your team is ready before the vehicle arrives.

  • Booking available day and night
  • Fewer missed calls and missed customers
  • Better planning for peak hours
DASHBOARD

Your entire business, live on one screen.

Open Vashq and see jobs, revenue and staff activity as they happen. No waiting for end-of-day numbers, no digging through paperwork.

  • Real-time view of operations and sales
  • Key numbers on a single screen
  • Check in on your wash from anywhere
AI ASSISTANT

Ask your business anything.

Open AI Insights to get a Full Report on your performance, or ask questions in plain language with Q&A. Clear answers in seconds, without building reports by hand.

  • Full Report for a complete performance summary
  • Q&A for quick, specific answers
  • Insights written in simple, usable language
REPORT SUMMARY

Clear reports. Confident decisions.

Understand your sales, services, staff and trends through clean, easy-to-read summaries. See what’s working, what isn’t and where to focus next.

  • Performance summaries at a glance
  • Growth tracked over time
  • Comparisons across services, staff and periods
CASH AND BANK BOOK

Every rupee in, every rupee out, accounted for.

Record cash and bank transactions along with daily expenses in one place. Understand where your money goes and what your business truly earns.

  • Cash, bank and expenses together
  • A clear daily and monthly financial picture
  • Fewer errors and easier reconciliation
INVENTORY MANAGEMENT

The right stock, always on the shelf.

Manage the car care products you sell, with stock updating as sales happen. Know what’s selling, what’s running low and when to reorder.

  • Real-time stock for retail products
  • Low-stock alerts
  • Insight into your best sellers
MULTIPLE BRANCHES

Many locations. One standard of excellence.

Manage every branch from a single account. Compare performance, track sales and staff by location and keep the same standards everywhere you operate.

  • All branches under one login
  • Branch-wise performance at a glance
  • Built to grow with your business
