Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 23, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VASHQ, a cloud-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform developed and operated by Wisemove Consultancy Pvt. Ltd. ("Company", "we", "our", or "us").

VASHQ is designed specifically for businesses in the car care industry, including car wash centers, detailing studios, ceramic coating centers, automobile service centers, and related businesses.

By accessing or using VASHQ, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

  • Business Email Address
  • Business Logo

User Information

  • Owner Name
  • Employee Details
  • Login Credentials
  • User Roles & Permissions

Customer Data

Businesses using VASHQ may store:

  • Customer Names
  • Mobile Numbers
